## Understanding Refunds

### What is a Refund?

A refund returns money to a client who purchased access to your gallery. When you issue a refund:

- The client receives their money back
- Their access to the gallery is revoked
- The transaction is marked as "Refunded" in your dashboard

### When to Consider a Refund

Common reasons for refunds:

- ✅ Client purchased the wrong gallery
- ✅ Technical issues prevented access
- ✅ Duplicate purchase by mistake
- ✅ Client is genuinely dissatisfied
- ✅ You and the client mutually agree

### Refund Time Limits

- **Full refunds**: Available within 30 days of purchase
- **Partial refunds**: Available within 30 days of purchase
- **After 30 days**: Contact support for special cases

### Financial Impact of Refunds

When you issue a refund:

```
Original Sale:     $29.99
Platform Fee:      -$3.00 (already deducted)
Your Earnings:     $26.99

After Full Refund:
Refund Amount:     -$29.99 (returned to client)
Your Balance:      -$26.99 (deducted from your balance)
```

> **Note**: The platform fee is not refunded to you. This covers the payment processing costs that have already occurred.

## Issuing a Refund

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### Step 1: Find the Sale

1. Go to **Revenue** in your dashboard
2. Click on the **Sales** tab
3. Find the sale you want to refund
4. Click on the sale to view details

![Screenshot placeholder: Sales table with sale selected]

#### Step 2: Click Refund

1. In the sale details, click the **Refund** button
2. The refund modal will appear

![Screenshot placeholder: Sale details with refund button]

#### Step 3: Choose Refund Type

Select the type of refund:

- **Full Refund**: Return the entire purchase amount
- **Partial Refund**: Return a portion of the purchase

![Screenshot placeholder: Refund type selection]

#### Step 4: Enter Refund Details

1. **Amount** (for partial refunds): Enter the amount to refund
2. **Reason**: Select or enter the reason for the refund
   - Client request
   - Technical issue
   - Duplicate purchase
   - Other (specify)

![Screenshot placeholder: Refund details form]

#### Step 5: Confirm the Refund

1. Review the refund details
2. Check the confirmation box
3. Click **Process Refund**

![Screenshot placeholder: Refund confirmation]

#### Step 6: Refund Processed

- The refund is processed immediately
- Client receives an email confirmation
- The sale status changes to "Refunded"
- Client's gallery access is revoked

### What Happens After a Refund

**For the Client:**
- Receives refund within 5-10 business days
- Gets email confirmation
- Loses access to the gallery
- Can repurchase if desired

**For You:**
- Sale marked as "Refunded" in dashboard
- Amount deducted from your balance
- Revenue metrics updated

## Partial Refunds

### When to Use Partial Refunds

Partial refunds are useful when:

- Client is partially satisfied
- You want to offer a goodwill gesture
- Resolving a minor complaint
- Offering a discount after purchase

### How Partial Refunds Work

```
Original Sale:     $29.99
Partial Refund:    $10.00

Client Receives:   $10.00 back
Client Keeps:      Access to gallery
Your New Earnings: $16.99 (original $26.99 - $10.00)
```

> **Important**: With partial refunds, the client typically keeps access to the gallery. You can choose to revoke access if appropriate.

### Issuing a Partial Refund

1. Follow the same steps as a full refund
2. Select **Partial Refund**
3. Enter the amount to refund
4. Choose whether to **Keep Access** or **Revoke Access**
5. Confirm and process

## Understanding Disputes

### What is a Dispute?

A dispute (also called a chargeback) occurs when a client contacts their bank or credit card company to reverse a charge. This is different from a refund request made directly to you.

### Why Disputes Happen

Common reasons:

- 🔴 Client doesn't recognize the charge
- 🔴 Client claims they didn't make the purchase
- 🔴 Client claims the product wasn't as described
- 🔴 Fraudulent use of the client's card
- 🔴 Client couldn't get a refund from you

### Dispute vs. Refund

| Aspect | Refund | Dispute |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Initiated by | You (the photographer) | Client's bank |
| Process | Instant | Takes 60-90 days |
| Fees | No additional fees | $15 dispute fee |
| Control | You decide | Bank decides |
| Relationship | Usually preserved | Often damaged |

### Financial Impact of Disputes

```
Original Sale:     $29.99
If Dispute Lost:
- Full amount returned to client
- $15 dispute fee charged to you
- Total cost: $44.99 + lost revenue
```

## Handling a Dispute

### When You Receive a Dispute

1. **You'll be notified** via email and in your dashboard
2. **A dispute alert** appears in your Revenue Dashboard
3. **You have limited time** to respond (usually 7-21 days)

![Screenshot placeholder: Dispute alert notification]

### Step-by-Step Response

#### Step 1: Review the Dispute

1. Go to **Revenue** → **Disputes**
2. Click on the dispute to view details
3. Note the reason and deadline

![Screenshot placeholder: Dispute details page]

#### Step 2: Gather Evidence

Collect documentation to support your case:

- ✅ Proof of delivery (access logs)
- ✅ Communication with the client
- ✅ Terms of service the client agreed to
- ✅ Screenshots of the gallery and paywall
- ✅ Client's email confirmation

#### Step 3: Submit Your Response

1. Click **Respond to Dispute**
2. You'll be redirected to Stripe Dashboard
3. Upload your evidence
4. Write a clear explanation
5. Submit before the deadline

#### Step 4: Wait for Resolution

- The bank reviews both sides
- Decision takes 60-90 days
- You'll be notified of the outcome

### Dispute Outcomes

**If You Win:**
- Funds returned to your account
- Dispute fee may be refunded
- No negative impact

**If You Lose:**
- Client keeps the refund
- You pay the $15 dispute fee
- Consider it a learning experience

### Tips for Winning Disputes

1. **Respond quickly**: Don't miss the deadline
2. **Be thorough**: Provide all relevant evidence
3. **Stay professional**: Avoid emotional language
4. **Show proof of delivery**: Access logs are powerful evidence
5. **Include terms**: Show the client agreed to your policies

## Preventing Disputes

### Best Practices

#### Clear Communication

- ✅ Describe exactly what clients will receive
- ✅ Show preview images before purchase
- ✅ State your refund policy clearly
- ✅ Use recognizable business name on statements

#### Easy Refund Process

- ✅ Make it easy for clients to request refunds
- ✅ Respond to refund requests quickly
- ✅ Be generous with legitimate requests
- ✅ A refund is better than a dispute

#### Recognizable Charges

The charge on your client's statement will show:
```
PIKSEND* [Your Business Name]
```

Make sure your business name is recognizable to clients.

#### Confirmation Emails

Ensure clients receive:
- Purchase confirmation email
- Access instructions
- Your contact information
- Refund policy reminder

### Red Flags to Watch For

Be cautious with:
- 🚩 Unusual purchase patterns
- 🚩 Multiple failed payment attempts
- 🚩 Requests to use different cards
- 🚩 Pressure to bypass normal process

## Refund Policy Best Practices

### Creating Your Policy

A good refund policy should be:

- **Clear**: Easy to understand
- **Fair**: Reasonable for both parties
- **Visible**: Shown before purchase
- **Consistent**: Applied equally to all clients

### Sample Refund Policy

```
REFUND POLICY

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

Full Refunds:
- Available within 7 days of purchase
- No questions asked
- Access will be revoked upon refund

Partial Refunds:
- Considered on a case-by-case basis
- Contact us to discuss

No Refunds After:
- 30 days from purchase date
- Photos have been downloaded

To request a refund, contact [your email].
```

### Displaying Your Policy

Show your refund policy:
- On the paywall screen
- In purchase confirmation emails
- In your gallery description
- On your website/portfolio

### Handling Refund Requests

**Do:**
- Respond within 24-48 hours
- Be empathetic and professional
- Ask for feedback
- Process legitimate requests quickly

**Don't:**
- Ignore requests
- Be defensive
- Make the process difficult
- Take it personally
