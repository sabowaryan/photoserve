## What is Stripe Connect?

Stripe Connect is a secure payment platform that allows you to receive payments directly from your clients. When a client purchases access to one of your galleries, the payment goes through Stripe and is deposited directly into your bank account.

### Benefits of Stripe Connect

- **Direct Payments**: Money goes straight to your bank account
- **Secure**: Industry-leading payment security
- **Automatic**: Payouts happen automatically on your schedule
- **Transparent**: See all your transactions in real-time
- **Global**: Accept payments from clients worldwide

## Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Access Stripe Connect Settings

1. Log in to your PikSend dashboard
2. Click on **Settings** in the left sidebar
3. Scroll down to the **Stripe Connect** section

![Screenshot placeholder: Settings page with Stripe Connect section highlighted]

### Step 2: Start the Connection Process

1. Click the **Connect with Stripe** button
2. You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure onboarding page

![Screenshot placeholder: Connect with Stripe button]

### Step 3: Create or Link Your Stripe Account

**If you're new to Stripe:**
1. Enter your email address
2. Create a password for your Stripe account
3. Click **Continue**

**If you already have a Stripe account:**
1. Click **Sign in** at the top of the page
2. Enter your existing Stripe credentials

### Step 4: Provide Your Business Information

Fill in the required information:

1. **Business Type**: Select "Individual" or "Company"
2. **Country**: Select your country of residence
3. **Business Details**: 
   - Legal business name (or your full name if individual)
   - Business address
   - Phone number

![Screenshot placeholder: Business information form]

### Step 5: Verify Your Identity

Stripe requires identity verification to comply with financial regulations:

1. Enter your **date of birth**
2. Enter the **last 4 digits of your SSN** (US) or equivalent ID number
3. You may be asked to upload a **photo of your ID**

> **Why is this required?** Identity verification helps prevent fraud and ensures compliance with financial regulations. Your information is securely stored by Stripe.

### Step 6: Add Your Bank Account

Enter your bank account details for receiving payouts:

1. **Account holder name**: Your name as it appears on the bank account
2. **Routing number**: Your bank's routing number
3. **Account number**: Your bank account number

![Screenshot placeholder: Bank account form]

> **Tip**: Double-check your bank details to avoid payout delays.

### Step 7: Review and Submit

1. Review all the information you've entered
2. Accept Stripe's Terms of Service
3. Click **Submit**

### Step 8: Return to PikSend

After completing the Stripe onboarding:

1. You'll be automatically redirected back to PikSend
2. Your account status will update to show your connection status
3. You may see "Pending" while Stripe verifies your information

![Screenshot placeholder: Connected account status]

## Understanding Your Account Status

Your Stripe Connect account can have different statuses:

### 🟢 Active

Your account is fully set up and ready to receive payments. You can:
- Enable paywalls on your galleries
- Receive payments from clients
- Get automatic payouts to your bank account

### 🟡 Pending

Stripe is reviewing your information. This usually takes:
- **Instant**: For most accounts
- **1-2 business days**: If additional verification is needed

While pending, you can still set up paywalls, but payments won't be processed until your account is active.

### 🔴 Restricted

Your account needs attention. Common reasons:
- Missing information
- Failed identity verification
- Bank account issues

**To resolve**: Click **Complete Setup** to provide the missing information.

### ⚪ Not Connected

You haven't connected a Stripe account yet. Click **Connect with Stripe** to get started.

## Managing Your Connected Account

### Viewing Your Stripe Dashboard

Once connected, you can access your Stripe dashboard directly:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Stripe Connect**
2. Click **View Stripe Dashboard**
3. You'll be redirected to Stripe where you can:
   - View all transactions
   - Manage payout settings
   - Update bank account information
   - Download tax documents

### Updating Your Information

To update your business or bank information:

1. Click **View Stripe Dashboard** in PikSend
2. Navigate to **Settings** in Stripe
3. Update the relevant information
4. Changes will sync automatically with PikSend

### Changing Payout Schedule

By default, payouts are sent automatically. To change your payout schedule:

1. Go to your Stripe Dashboard
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Payouts**
3. Choose your preferred schedule:
   - **Daily**: Receive payouts every business day
   - **Weekly**: Receive payouts once per week
   - **Monthly**: Receive payouts once per month

### Disconnecting Your Account

If you need to disconnect your Stripe account:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Stripe Connect**
2. Click **Disconnect Account**
3. Confirm the disconnection

> **Warning**: Disconnecting will disable all paywalls on your galleries. Any pending payouts will still be processed.

## Troubleshooting

### "My account is stuck on Pending"

**Solution**: 
1. Check your email for messages from Stripe
2. Log in to your Stripe Dashboard to see if additional information is needed
3. Contact Stripe support if the issue persists after 48 hours

### "I can't complete the onboarding process"

**Common causes**:
- Browser blocking pop-ups (allow pop-ups for stripe.com)
- Incomplete information
- Unsupported country

**Solution**: Try using a different browser or clearing your cache.

### "My bank account was rejected"

**Common causes**:
- Incorrect routing or account number
- Account type not supported (e.g., savings account in some countries)
- Name mismatch between Stripe and bank account

**Solution**: Double-check your bank details and try again.

### "I'm not receiving payouts"

**Check the following**:
1. Your account status is "Active"
2. Your bank account is verified
3. You have a positive balance
4. Your payout schedule hasn't been paused

### "I need to change my connected account"

To switch to a different Stripe account:
1. Disconnect your current account
2. Connect with the new Stripe account
3. Note: This will temporarily disable paywalls
