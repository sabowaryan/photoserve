## What is a Gallery Paywall?

A gallery paywall allows you to sell access to your photo galleries. When enabled, clients must purchase access before they can view and download your high-resolution photos.

### How It Works

1. **You set a price** for your gallery
2. **Client visits** your gallery link
3. **Client sees** a preview or paywall screen
4. **Client pays** through secure Stripe checkout
5. **Client gets** instant access to all HD photos
6. **You receive** payment (minus platform fee)

---

## Paywall Types

PikSend offers two paywall modes to fit different selling strategies:

### Full Paywall Mode

The client sees a locked screen with:
- Gallery title and description
- 3-5 blurred preview images
- Price and purchase button
- List of what's included

**Best for**: Wedding galleries, event photography, exclusive content

![Screenshot placeholder: Full paywall screen]

### Freemium Preview Mode

The client can browse the entire gallery with:
- All photos visible in low resolution (800px)
- Watermarks on all images
- Downloads disabled
- Sticky banner to unlock HD access

**Best for**: Portfolio showcases, letting clients see before buying

![Screenshot placeholder: Freemium preview mode]

## Setting Up Your First Paywall

### Prerequisites

Before setting up a paywall, ensure:
- ✅ You have a **Pro plan** subscription
- ✅ Your **Stripe account is connected** and active
- ✅ You have a **gallery with photos** uploaded

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### Step 1: Open Gallery Settings

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click on **Galleries** in the sidebar
3. Select the gallery you want to monetize
4. Click on the **Monetization** tab

![Screenshot placeholder: Gallery settings with Monetization tab]

#### Step 2: Enable the Paywall

1. Toggle **Enable Paywall** to ON
2. The monetization options will appear

![Screenshot placeholder: Enable paywall toggle]

#### Step 3: Set Your Price

1. Enter your desired price (e.g., $29.99)
2. Select your currency (USD, EUR, or CAD)
3. The calculator will show your earnings after fees

![Screenshot placeholder: Price input and calculator]

#### Step 4: Choose Preview Mode

Select how clients will see your gallery before purchasing:

- **Full Paywall**: Clients see a locked screen with blurred previews
- **Freemium Preview**: Clients can browse low-res images with watermarks

![Screenshot placeholder: Preview mode selector]

#### Step 5: Configure Additional Options

**If using Freemium Preview:**
- ✅ **Show Watermark**: Add your watermark to preview images
- ✅ **Disable Downloads**: Prevent downloading preview images

**Access Duration (Optional):**
- Leave empty for **lifetime access** (recommended)
- Or set a specific number of days (e.g., 30 days)

#### Step 6: Review and Save

1. Review the earnings calculator:
   - **Sale Price**: $29.99
   - **Platform Fee (10%)**: $3.00
   - **Your Earnings**: $26.99

2. Click **Save Changes**

3. Your paywall is now active!

#### Step 7: Share Your Gallery

1. Copy your gallery link
2. Share it with your clients via email, text, or social media
3. When they visit, they'll see the paywall

## Pricing Your Gallery

### Pricing Guidelines

| Gallery Type | Suggested Price Range |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Mini Session (10-20 photos) | $15 - $35 |
| Portrait Session (30-50 photos) | $25 - $75 |
| Event Coverage (50-100 photos) | $35 - $99 |
| Wedding Gallery (200+ photos) | $75 - $250 |
| Commercial/Corporate | $100 - $500 |

### Pricing Limits

- **Minimum price**: $5.00
- **Maximum price**: $500.00

### Pricing Tips

1. **Consider your market**: Research what other photographers in your area charge
2. **Value your work**: Don't undervalue your photography
3. **Factor in the experience**: Clients pay for convenience and quality
4. **Test different prices**: You can adjust prices anytime

## Preview Mode (Freemium)

The freemium preview mode lets clients browse your entire gallery before purchasing. This can increase conversions by letting clients see exactly what they're buying.

### How Freemium Works

| Feature | Before Purchase | After Purchase |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| View Photos | ✅ Low-res (800px) | ✅ Full HD |
| Watermarks | ✅ Visible | ❌ Removed |
| Download | ❌ Disabled | ✅ Enabled |
| Slideshow | ✅ Available | ✅ Available |

### Setting Up Freemium Mode

1. Enable the paywall on your gallery
2. Select **Freemium Preview** as the preview mode
3. Configure watermark settings:
   - Use your custom watermark (if uploaded)
   - Or use the default PikSend watermark
4. Save your changes

### Freemium Best Practices

- **Use a subtle watermark**: Don't obscure the photo completely
- **Include your branding**: Use your logo as the watermark
- **Position strategically**: Center or corner placement works best

## Understanding Fees

### Fee Structure

When a client purchases your gallery, here's how the payment is split:

| Component | Percentage | Example ($29.99 sale) |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| Your Earnings | 90% | $26.99 |
| Platform Fee | 10% | $3.00 |

The platform fee covers:
- Stripe payment processing fees
- PikSend platform costs
- Customer support

### Example Calculations

**$29.99 Gallery Sale:**
```
Sale Price:        $29.99
Platform Fee:      -$3.00 (10%)
─────────────────────────
Your Earnings:     $26.99
```

**$99.00 Wedding Gallery:**
```
Sale Price:        $99.00
Platform Fee:      -$9.90 (10%)
─────────────────────────
Your Earnings:     $89.10
```

**$199.00 Premium Package:**
```
Sale Price:        $199.00
Platform Fee:      -$19.90 (10%)
─────────────────────────
Your Earnings:     $179.10
```

### When You Get Paid

- Payments are processed immediately when a client purchases
- Funds are transferred to your Stripe account
- Payouts to your bank follow your Stripe payout schedule (daily, weekly, or monthly)

## Best Practices

### Gallery Presentation

1. **Use a compelling title**: "Sarah & John's Wedding - June 2025" is better than "Wedding Photos"
2. **Add a description**: Tell clients what's included
3. **Choose great preview images**: Select your best 3-5 photos for the paywall preview
4. **Set a cover photo**: This appears in the paywall and when sharing

### Pricing Strategy

1. **Start with market rates**: Research your local market
2. **Offer value**: More photos = higher price is justified
3. **Consider urgency**: Time-limited access can encourage quick purchases
4. **Bundle services**: Offer prints or albums as upsells

### Client Communication

1. **Explain the process**: Let clients know they'll need to purchase access
2. **Highlight benefits**: HD quality, no watermarks, unlimited downloads
3. **Provide support**: Be available to answer questions
4. **Send reminders**: Follow up if clients haven't purchased

### Technical Tips

1. **Test your paywall**: View your gallery as a client would
2. **Check mobile experience**: Many clients will view on phones
3. **Verify Stripe connection**: Ensure your account is active before sharing

## FAQ

### Can I change the price after setting it?

Yes! You can update the price anytime. Changes apply to new purchases only - existing purchases keep their original price.

### What happens if I disable the paywall?

The gallery becomes free to access. Any clients who already purchased will still have access.

### Can I offer discounts?

Currently, discount codes are not supported. You can manually adjust the price for special offers.

### What currencies are supported?

PikSend supports:
- USD (US Dollar)
- EUR (Euro)
- CAD (Canadian Dollar)

### Can clients pay with any payment method?

Stripe accepts:
- Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Bank transfers (in some regions)

### What if a client has trouble paying?

1. Ask them to try a different payment method
2. Check if their card is valid
3. Suggest using a different browser
4. Contact support if issues persist

### Can I see who purchased my gallery?

Yes! The Revenue Dashboard shows all purchases including:
- Client email
- Purchase date
- Amount paid
- Payment status

### How do I handle refunds?

See our [Refunds and Disputes Guide](./refunds-disputes.md) for detailed instructions.
